Privacy policy

ShortsForge is a personal tool. It is used by one person, its owner, to post the owner's own videos to the owner's own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts.

  • It asks only for the permissions needed to read the account's basic profile and to upload videos.
  • Login tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in the operating system's credential store. They are never sent anywhere except to the platform they belong to.
  • It collects no data about other people, uses no analytics or cookies, and shares no data with anyone.
  • To remove its access, disconnect the app in the platform's settings (apps and websites / connected apps). Deleting the app's tokens from the computer removes everything ShortsForge holds.

Contact: the owner of this site, through the GitHub repository that hosts it.
